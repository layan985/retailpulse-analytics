# Business memo

## Decision question
Which parts of the customer and product system should an analyst investigate first if the goal is to grow repeat revenue without destroying margin?

## Recommended decision frame
Do not maximize a single KPI. Read revenue together with margin, returns, repeat rate, and acquisition-channel economics. A discount can raise basket revenue while simultaneously reducing contribution and increasing returns. A channel can produce a large share of revenue simply because it has more customers, while another channel has stronger revenue per customer.

## Model use
Use repeat-purchase probabilities for prioritization and measurement design, not as proof that contacting a customer will cause a purchase. Uplift requires an experiment or a causal design.
